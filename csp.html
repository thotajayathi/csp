Pension Schemes

Agricultural Support

Education Support

Healthcare Support

Employment Support

Village Development Recommendations

1. Drinking Water

Improve the reliability of water supply and address shortages reported by residents.

2. Roads and Drainage

Inspect damaged roads, clean drains regularly and address wastewater stagnation.

3. Farmer Support

Improve awareness of eligible agricultural assistance and address irrigation concerns.

4. Welfare Scheme Awareness

Help residents understand scheme eligibility, required documents and application procedures.

5. Education and Healthcare

Review residents' concerns and identify areas where public services need improvement.

6. Employment Opportunities

Promote awareness of suitable skill development, livelihood and employment programmes.

Recommendations should be prioritized after reviewing actual survey responses and consulting the relevant local authorities.
